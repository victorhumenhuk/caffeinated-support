Caffeinated

Dial in every cup. Log a brew, tap how it tasted, and see exactly what to change next time.

Caffeinated is a coffee journal for people who brew at home, from espresso to pour-over, AeroPress, French press, moka pot and cold brew. You add the bags on your shelf, brew with a guided timer, and rate each cup on a taste compass that runs from sour to bitter and from weak to strong. The next brew starts from what the last one taught you.

Today

The first card says which coffee to brew next and how it's doing since its roast date, then how much caffeine you've had today and roughly when it will drop below 50 mg. Brew again starts the timer with last time's numbers already filled in.

Beans

Every bag on your shelf shows its days off roast, whether it's still resting, at its best or past its peak, and how many grams are left. Each brew takes its dose out of the bag, so you see when it's time to order more.

Brew

Fifteen recipes across nine methods, each with timed steps and the water to pour to at every stage. The timer runs on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, on your Apple Watch, and full screen on an iPad on the counter.

Journal

Every brew and every coffee you buy at a café, with a map of the cafés you visit. You can bring your history over from Beanconqueror and export everything as a CSV file at any time.

Start for free

The free plan has no time limit. It covers the timer, every built-in recipe, logging and rating, caffeine tracking with Apple Health, the café log, the Watch app, import and export, for up to three coffees on your shelf at once. Caffeinated Pro adds the dial-in coach, which names one exact change after each brew, plus bag scanning, Insights, your own recipes, Bluetooth scales, iCloud sync and no limit on coffees or equipment.

Your data stays with you

Caffeinated has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and you don't need an account. What you enter stays on your device, and in your own iCloud only if you turn on sync.
